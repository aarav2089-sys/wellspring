Relaxation Techniques


Simple relaxation methods can help calm the mind and reduce tension. Taking a few minutes each day to relax can improve focus, mood, and emotional balance.

  • Practice deep breathing
  • Try meditation or mindfulness
  • Spend time in nature
  • Listen to calming music
